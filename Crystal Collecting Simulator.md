## What is Crystal Collecting Simulator?

It’s a simulator on Roblox. Crystals drop from the sky into different areas, you mine them with a pickaxe, and every area spawns its own mix of rarities. Free to join, made by LumeCraftors.

### Mine what falls

Walk up to a crystal, hold the mining prompt, and swing your pickaxe until it breaks.

### Watch your pickaxe

The durability bar shifts from green to yellow to red. When it hits zero, the pickaxe is destroyed.

### Restock and upgrade

The Pickaxe Shop refreshes on a timer. Buy with in-game cash, or use Robux for an optional shortcut.

### Keep your haul sorted

A hotbar inventory with categories, drag and drop, and favorites keeps your finds easy to reach.

## You can be mining in under a minute

1. **Open the game on Roblox**Use the Play button on this page, or search for Crystal Collecting Simulator in Roblox.
2. **Equip your pickaxe**Select it on your hotbar so it’s in your hand.
3. **Find a fallen crystal**Crystals drop from the sky into each area. Walk up to one.
4. **Hold the mining prompt**Keep holding while you swing. When the crystal breaks, it goes into your inventory.
5. **Check durability, then restock**Each swing wears the pickaxe down. Visit the Pickaxe Shop before it breaks.

## Eight rarities, one goal

Every find pops up with its rarity and the area it came from. Tap a gem to look closer.

## Questions

Is Crystal Collecting Simulator free?

Yes, it’s free to join on Roblox. The Pickaxe Shop also offers optional Robux purchases, which are handled by Roblox.

How does mining work?

Walk up to a crystal and hold the mining prompt. Your character swings the pickaxe until the crystal breaks and drops into your inventory.

What happens when my pickaxe breaks?

A pickaxe is destroyed when its durability reaches zero. The bar turns red as it runs low, so restock in the Pickaxe Shop before then.

How often does the shop restock?

On a timer. The countdown is shown at the top of the Pickaxe Shop window.

Do all areas drop the same crystals?

No. Each area has its own weighted mix of Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Secret, Exclusive, and Limited crystals.
